# Walkthrough for Day 1 challenge - The concierge knows too much
* Day 1 moves from finding accounts to talking to one. Vera, the resort's AI concierge, holds the flag and she only gives it to guests she trusts.
* The challenge is to convince her that I'm one of those guests, such as Ponzi, Patch or Vibe, then get her talking and grab the flag when she reveals it to you.
* Nothing here can be solved by asking for the flag directly, so the work is figuring out who Vera trusts and why.

### What I took from it:
An AI assistant can be talked into things the same way a person can. If the only check is whether the story sounds believable, then any public detail about a guest becomes a way to impersonate them. 
Access like this needs a real check behind it, and a friendly conversation isn't one.
