#walkthrough for day 0 challenge-The Brochure

*The challenge starts with a brochure for Byte Lotus Resorts and one goal: find the other profile that holds the flag.
*The brochure has a few lines worth noting. The tagline says a polished first impression can still leave a trail.
*One panel says some things aren't posted but some clues are, and tells you to find them on `Instagram "or not"` . Another panel mentions a `concierge named Vera` who can give further information.
*I took the resort name as the username and searched for it on 'https://instantusername.com/', which checks one username across many social platforms at once. The Instagram account came back as registered, so I opened it.
*On the account I went through the posts and the profile for clues. The brochure hints at the concierge, so I looked at who the account followed. The following list included the concierge's account whose posts had the flag.
#Pro-tip:
What I took from it: a company's official page rarely holds the secret itself, but its follows, tags and mentions can lead to the people and accounts that do.
Username search tools make the first step quick and reading the brochure closely told me where to look next.
